# 5ire (5ireChain)

> 5ire is a layer-1, EVM-compatible smart contract blockchain built on the Polkadot SDK (Substrate), founded in 2021 and headquartered in Dubai with engineering in India. Its Sustainable Proof of Stake / "Proof of 5ire" consensus weights validator selection and rewards by ESG performance, aligned to the UN Sustainable Development Goals. Developers reach the network over a standard, unauthenticated Ethereum JSON-RPC endpoint (mainnet chain ID 995, Thunder testnet chain ID 997) with unmodified Ethereum tooling, or over the native Substrate/WASM RPC via the Polkadot-derived @5ire/api client.

## Networks

- [5ireChain Mainnet JSON-RPC](https://rpc.5ire.network): Ethereum JSON-RPC 2.0, EIP-155 chain ID 995, native currency 5ire (18 decimals). Public and unauthenticated.
- [5ireChain Thunder Testnet JSON-RPC](https://rpc.testnet.5ire.network): Ethereum JSON-RPC 2.0, EIP-155 chain ID 997, native currency T5IRE. Faucet at https://testnet.5irescan.io/faucet.
- [5ireChain Native Substrate RPC](https://rpc.5ire.network): Polkadot-SDK RPC over WebSocket (node default port 9944) for WASM contracts, staking and the ESG pallets.
- [Chain registry — 995](https://chainid.network/chain/995/)
- [Chain registry — 997](https://chainid.network/chain/997/)
